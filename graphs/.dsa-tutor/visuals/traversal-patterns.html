Which traversal? — the decision tell

BFS "shortest / fewest steps / minimum moves" in an unweighted world, or "process level by level". Spreads in rings.
DFS "does a path exist", "explore/enumerate all paths", "count connected groups", "detect a cycle", "topological order", anything recursive on a tree. Dives deep.
